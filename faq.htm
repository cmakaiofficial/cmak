Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What is Cmak?

Cmak is an online tool that converts your images into print-ready PDF files with options like bleed, crop marks, DPI, and color settings.

2. Do I need to install any software?

No. Cmak works directly in your web browser without installing any software.

3. Is Cmak free to use?

Yes. Basic features are free. Additional premium features may be available in the future.

4. Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Your files are processed locally in your browser whenever possible, helping keep your data private.

5. Which file formats are supported?

Cmak supports common image formats such as JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP.

6. How can I contact Cmak?

You can visit the Contact Us page or email our support team for assistance.
